Choices I made overnight
  1. The Log tab is Ekle ("Add"). "Kaydet" is already the Save button.
  2. The Journal tab is Takvim ("Calendar"). "Günlük" also means "daily", as in the daily budget.
  3. Meals are written in full: Kahvaltı, Öğle yemeği, Akşam yemeği, Ara öğün. They fit everywhere I checked.
  4. A name that is filled in goes after a colon or before "için", so Turkish never needs an ending on it.
  5. The percent sign goes first in Turkish: %30.
  6. No thousands separator in either language, as today: 1850 kcal.
  7. English screens today show 72,5. The plan switches English to 72.5, including the help text and the food portions.
  8. "kcal" stays "kcal" in Turkish.
  9. Food pictures appear as letter tiles on this page only. The app keeps its food icons.
  10. Built-in food names follow what the values are, even where people search for something else (cheddar is "Çedar peyniri", and searching "kaşar" still finds it).
